## Eligibility Quick Filters

### By Age
- **Age 18+**: RIS, AGR, Chômage, Most benefits
- **Age 21+**: Allocation Handicapées
- **Age 25+**: AGR (special rate), GRAPA eligibility
- **Age 62-67**: Pension de Retraite (age-dependent)
- **Age 65+**: GRAPA
- **Age 0-18**: Family Allowances, Child-specific benefits
- **Students <25**: Allocations d'Études

### By Employment Status
- **Employed Full-time**: Chèques Repas, Tax benefits, Crédit Temps, Insurance
- **Part-time**: AGR (primary), Tax benefits
- **Unemployed**: Allocations de Chômage, RIS, Formation
- **Self-employed**: Different rules (Pension, Congé Maternité lump sum)
- **Students**: Allocations d'Études, Family Allowance

### By Family Status
- **Single**: RIS €1,070/month, Individual rates
- **Cohabitant**: RIS €713/month, Reduced rates
- **Parent**: RIS €1,450/month, Family Allowances, Congé Parental
- **Pregnant**: Congé Maternité
- **With children 0-25**: Allocations Familiales, Childcare support

### By Income Level
- **No income**: RIS, Allocation Handicapées, GRAPA (means-tested)
- **Low income**: AGR, Housing benefits, Energy tariff
- **Moderate income**: Some family benefits conditional
- **High income**: Tax deductions primary benefit

## Cumulation Rules (Key)

### Usually ALLOWED Together
- RIS + Small work income (€252/month exemption)
- Allocations Familiales + Work income
- Pension + Small supplementary income
- Chèques Repas + other benefits (employer-provided)

### Usually FORBIDDEN Together
- RIS + Allocations de Chômage (RIS is fallback)
- RIS + GRAPA (choose one)
- AGR + Full unemployment (AGR requires part-time work)
- Allocations de Chômage + Crédit Temps working (employment status issue)

### CONDITIONAL Combinations
- Pension de Retraite + Part-time work (earnings limits apply)
- GRAPA + Small pension (pension reduces GRAPA amount)
- Allocations Familiales + Student income (limits may apply)

## Key Threshold Values (2024-2025)

### Income Thresholds
- **RIS Max Income (Individual)**: Varies by category + resources
- **AGR Max Salary**: €2,111.89/month gross (2025)
- **GRAPA Income Limit**: Earnings cap (reduces benefit)
- **Housing Aid Max Income**: €25,000/year (varies by region)

### Patrimony Limits
- **RIS Movable Property**: €6,200 max
- **RIS Immovable (Residence)**: €12,500 max (cadastral value)
- **GRAPA Movable**: €16,500 max
- **GRAPA Housing**: Primary residence excluded

### Duration Limits
- **Unemployment**: 48 months max (age-dependent tiers)
- **Crédit Temps**: 12-60 months (reason-dependent)
- **Congé Parental**: 12-24 months
- **Maternity Leave**: 8 weeks standard

## Application Process Overview

### Typical Process
1. **Verification** - Check eligibility criteria
2. **Documentation** - Gather required documents
3. **Application** - Submit to appropriate administration
4. **Processing** - Investigation period (days to weeks)
5. **Decision** - Approval or appeal
6. **Retroactivity** - Some benefits apply from application date
7. **Review** - Periodic check-ups (quarterly to annual)

### Key Documents Needed
- Identity proof (ID card, passport)
- Residency proof (utility bill, lease)
- Income proof (pay stubs, tax return)
- Family status (birth certificate, marriage cert)
- Medical proof (for disability/health benefits)
- Employment proof (contract, ONEM certificate)
