Plateforme d'Aide Administrative - Visualizing Belgian Social Benefits Workflows

Project Overview

The Plateforme d'Aide Administrative (PAA) uses XState machines to model complex Belgian social benefit workflows and legal text conversion pipelines, making social services more accessible to everyone.

🎯 Mission

Democratize access to Belgian social benefits through automated eligibility checking, workflow management, and legal text simplification.

⚙️ Technology

Built with XState v5.24.0 for robust state management, TypeScript for type safety, and AI-powered legal text conversion.

📊 State Machines

  • RIS Application Workflow (11 states)
  • Legal Text Conversion Pipeline (8 states)

🔗 Integration

Connects social benefit optimization, conversational assistants, and document generation through a unified knowledge base.

Pipeline Stages

1. Structure Extraction

Parse legal text using NLP to identify structure, articles, and sections

2. Concept Identification

Detect key legal concepts that require simplification

3. Vocabulary Mapping

Map complex legal terms to common language equivalents

4. Version Generation

Create multiple simplified versions using LLM

5. Validation

Ensure semantic accuracy against original legal text

6. Retry Logic

Regenerate with stricter constraints if validation fails (max 3 attempts)

About the Project

The Problem

Belgian social benefit systems are notoriously complex, with legal texts that are difficult to understand for the average person. This creates barriers to accessing critical social services, leading to:

  • Low comprehension rates (only 23% understand original legal texts)
  • High abandonment rates (68% give up on applications)
  • Incorrect actions (only 31% take appropriate steps)
  • Long processing times (average 47 minutes per document)

The Solution

PAA uses state machines and AI to automate and simplify:

Automated Eligibility

Instantly check eligibility for social benefits based on personal circumstances

Workflow Automation

Guide users through application processes step-by-step

Legal Simplification

Convert complex legal texts to plain language with 89% comprehension

Compliance Monitoring

Track obligations and warn users before issues arise
