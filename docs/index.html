Plateforme d'Aide Administrative - Visualizing Belgian Social Benefits Workflows

Project Overview

The Plateforme d'Aide Administrative (PAA) uses XState machines to model complex Belgian social benefit workflows and legal text conversion pipelines, making social services more accessible to everyone.

🎯 Mission

Democratize access to Belgian social benefits through automated eligibility checking, workflow management, and legal text simplification.

⚙️ Technology

Built with XState v5.24.0 for robust state management, TypeScript for type safety, and AI-powered legal text conversion.

📊 State Machines

  • RIS Application Workflow (11 states)
  • Legal Text Conversion Pipeline (8 states)

🔗 Integration

Connects social benefit optimization, conversational assistants, and document generation through a unified knowledge base.

Manages the complete lifecycle of RIS (Revenu d'Intégration Sociale - Social Integration Income) applications, from initial eligibility checking through active benefit management and compliance monitoring.
